Welcome to Trendbin: Your Live Internet Dashboard

The internet moves fast. What goes viral at 9:00 AM might be forgotten by noon. Trendbin was built to solve the modern digital dilemma of information overload by aggregating the top performing content across the web's biggest platforms in one clean, real-time dashboard.

How Trendbin Works

Our automated systems plug directly into the official developer APIs of major networks. Whether you are looking for the most-watched videos on YouTube, the fastest-growing open-source projects on GitHub, or the latest cultural conversations on Bluesky, Trendbin compiles the raw data and updates every single hour.

Why Track Cross-Platform Trends?

For digital marketers, content creators, and developers, knowing what is capturing human attention is vital. By comparing what trends on a video platform versus a text-based network, you can spot emerging cultural shifts before they become mainstream news. Trendbin acts as your digital radar, allowing you to filter out the noise and focus purely on what people are actually engaging with.
