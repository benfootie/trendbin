The internet moves incredibly fast. What goes viral at 9:00 AM on a Tuesday might be completely forgotten by Wednesday afternoon. 

As digital culture fragments across different platforms, tracking what people actually care about has become a full-time job. Are people watching gaming streams on YouTube? Are they deploying new AI repositories on GitHub? What is the current conversation on Bluesky?

### Why We Built Trendbin
We built Trendbin to solve this information overload. Instead of opening five different apps and navigating through algorithms designed to keep you scrolling, Trendbin plugs directly into the official developer APIs of the web's biggest platforms. 

By aggregating real-time data from **YouTube, Spotify, GitHub, and Bluesky**, we provide a clean, noise-free dashboard of human attention.

### What to Expect from this Blog
Over the coming weeks, we will be using this space to analyze the data we collect. We will dive deep into questions like:
* How long does a viral song stay on the Spotify Global Top 50?
* Do tech trends on GitHub predict broader consumer tech trends?
* What are the most common traits of videos that hit #1 on YouTube?

Stay tuned, and make sure to check the main dashboard to see what the internet is obsessed with right now.
